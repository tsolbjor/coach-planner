# Proposed issues: Match and rotation planning for volunteer youth coaches: practice and comparable apps

Proposed by the researcher from [the brief](2026-11-16-match-and-rotation-planning-for-volunteer-youth-coaches-prac.md). None of them is filed: each `## ` heading below is one proposal, with an optional `Labels:` line and then its text. Edit or delete them here; what is left when this is merged is filed as issues, on the word of whoever the team says approves that.

## Add a parent-friendly share format (image or read-only summary) for the plan
Labels: discovery, sharing
Apps in this niche pitch the plan as something to show parents (a playing-time report, a no-login parent link), and team-app comparisons advise against moving parents to a new app. Consider a share output that pastes well into chat groups (for example an image or clean text of the plan) alongside the import URL, as input to #38. Rests on https://fairtime.football/ and https://clipitcoach.com/blog/best-sports-lineup-apps (retrieved 2026-11-16).

## Decide whether to support live in-match substitution tracking
Labels: discovery
Every comparable rotation app collected offers a match clock with per-player minutes and re-planning after injury or late arrival, and vendors present this as the main reason to leave paper and spreadsheets. The product owner should decide whether coach-planner stays a pre-match planner or adds this. Rests on https://pitch-planner.app/soccer-substitution-tracker-comparison/ and https://www.subnowapp.com/ (2026-11-16).

## Check that late arrivals and injuries can be handled by re-planning the remaining match
Labels: discovery
SubNow and Coach Meister advertise recalculating the plan when players arrive late or are injured. Verify how the scheduler's locked history and pins support this, and whether the flow is quick enough on a sideline. Rests on https://www.subnowapp.com/ (2026-11-16).

## Support several teams and per-team squads
Labels: enhancement
A Norwegian CoachAny review asks for several teams, and the Norwegian open-source spilletid keeps separate squads and minutes per team. Check whether coach-planner covers coaches with two teams. One review is thin evidence. Rests on https://apps.apple.com/no/app/coachany/id987231566 and https://github.com/williamwmy/spilletid (2026-11-16).

## Show season or tournament minutes per player
Labels: enhancement
Pitch Planner and the spilletid app give cumulative minutes across matches, and Fairtime builds its pitch around a season record. Consider whether coach-planner should surface this across plans. Rests on https://pitch-planner.app/ and https://fairtime.football/ (2026-11-16).

## Research handball rotation needs directly
Labels: discovery
The collected pages show only lineup and tactics apps for handball and no dedicated rotation planner, and nothing on how coaches of under-15 handball teams handle playing time. A short conversation with a few Norwegian handball coaches would settle whether the scheduler fits. Rests on https://apps.apple.com/us/app/handballteamcreator/id6747158405 (2026-11-16).
